Academic & Technical Projects

Renewable Energy & Embedded Systems

Smart Solar Priority Power Management System

Overview: An automated hybrid energy management system designed to eliminate rural and semi-urban power outages by intelligently prioritizing power sources to maximize solar energy usage and minimize electricity costs.

Problem Addressed: Frequent power cuts and heavy dependency on costly, polluting grid electricity and diesel generators in remote and semi-urban areas.
SDG Alignment: SDG 7 (Affordable & Clean Energy) | SDG 13 (Climate Action)
5-Tier Priority Switching Logic: 1. Solar (Day/Primary) → 2. Battery Inverter (Night, >20% SOC) → 3. AC Grid (Night, <20% SOC) → 4. Generator (Power Cut) → 5. Auto-Switch back to Solar.
Technical Domain & Tools: Embedded Systems, C Programming, Power Electronics, Charge Controllers & Relay Switching, MATLAB/Simulink & Wokwi Simulation.
Key Features: Zero-overlap priority logic, automated safe AC switching (no manual intervention), real-time battery & grid telemetry, scalable rural deployment design.
Technology Readiness Level: TRL 2–3 — Concept Formulated & Proof-of-Concept Prototype Validated.
