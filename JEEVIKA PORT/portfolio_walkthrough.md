# Jeevika R - Professional Engineering Portfolio & CV

A modern, responsive, and ATS-optimized portfolio website created for **Jeevika R**, an **Electrical and Electronics Engineering (EEE)** student.

---

## 🌟 Key Highlights & Features

- **Strict Adherence to Information Integrity**: Uses strictly the personal details provided without inventing fake marks, achievements, companies, or experience. All missing fields are clearly designated with standard editable placeholders (e.g., `[Add College / University Name]`).
- **12 Comprehensive Portfolio Sections**:
  1. **Hero Section**: Displays Jeevika's name, ECE degree title, exact requested introduction, call-to-action buttons, and interactive PCB tech snippet visual.
  2. **About Me**: Concise professional overview highlighting interest in electronics, embedded systems, emerging technologies, practical projects, and continuous professional growth.
  3. **Education**: Dedicated timeline card with placeholders for Degree, Institution, Department, Year of Study, Graduation Year, and CGPA/Percentage.
  4. **Technical Skills**: Structured categories for Programming Languages, Embedded Systems, Electronics Core, Software/Simulation Tools, and Additional Technical Knowledge.
  5. **Projects**: Structured cards for Project Title, Short Description, Problem Addressed, Technologies/Tools, Role, Key Features, Outcome, and Demo Links.
  6. **Internships / Experience**: Clean placeholder card for Organization, Role, Duration, Responsibilities, and Skills Gained.
  7. **Certifications**: Dedicated cards for Certification Name, Issuing Body, Date, and Credential Links.
  8. **Achievements**: Academic, technical, competition, or extracurricular achievements section.
  9. **Workshops & Technical Training**: Dedicated section for technical workshops and hands-on training.
  10. **Soft Skills**: Professional badges for Communication, Teamwork, Time Management, Problem Solving, and Adaptability.
  11. **Ambition / Career Interest**: Exact required quote: *"My ambition is to build a successful career in the electronics and technology field, continuously improve my knowledge and skills, and contribute to practical and innovative solutions."*
  12. **ATS-Friendly Professional Resume**: Built-in 1-page paper format stripped of tables/graphics for maximum Applicant Tracking System (ATS) readability.
- **Interactive Live Details Customizer Drawer**: Click the **"Fill Details"** button in the header or resume section to fill in your phone number, email, college name, CGPA, and social profiles. Updates reflect **instantly** on both the portfolio and ATS resume!
- **1-Click ATS PDF Export & Print**: Click **"Download Resume"** or **"Print / Save as ATS PDF"** to generate a clean, single-page print document formatted for job applications.
- **Copy Raw Text Button**: Instantly copy formatted plain text of the resume to paste into online job portals.
- **Dark & Light Mode Switcher**: Toggle between a sleek Cyber ECE Dark Theme (Cyan/Teal accents) and a crisp Executive Light Theme (Slate Blue accents).

## 🚀 How to View and Use Your Portfolio

### 1. Open in any Web Browser
Simply double-click `index.html` or right-click `index.html` and choose **Open with > Chrome / Edge / Firefox**.

### 2. Customizing Your Placeholders Live
1. Open `index.html` in your browser.
2. Click the **"Fill Details"** button in the top navigation bar.
3. Type in your College Name, Phone Number, Email, LinkedIn, GitHub, Year of Study, etc.
4. Click **"Save & Apply to Portfolio"** — your portfolio and ATS resume will update immediately!

### 3. Downloading Your ATS PDF Resume
1. Navigate to the **ATS Resume** section or click **"Download Resume"** in the Hero header.
2. Click **"Print / Save as ATS PDF"**.
3. In the browser print popup, select **"Save as PDF"** as your destination and save your one-page ATS resume!
